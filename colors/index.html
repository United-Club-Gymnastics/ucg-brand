Not part of the everyday palette. Never for official communications, wayfinding or UI.

Every brand text color on every brand background, with its WCAG (the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, the international standard for accessible web content) contrast ratio. UCG rule: AA (WCAG's standard level: text needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against its background, large text 3:1) everywhere. Body text needs 4.5:1. Large text (24px+ regular, 18.5px+ bold), icons and logos need 3:1.

Quick list: safe for body text

  • On Navy Blue (#1E2B38): Blue Green, Light Blue, Purple, Red Orange, Golden Yellow, White
  • On Dark Blue Green (#184B56): Blue Green, Light Blue, Purple, Golden Yellow, White
  • On Blue Green (#A5C8CF): Navy Blue, Dark Blue Green
  • On Light Blue (#DBEBEE): Navy Blue, Dark Blue Green
  • On Purple (#ADBAE9): Navy Blue, Dark Blue Green
  • On Red Orange (#F4694A): Navy Blue
  • On Golden Yellow (#F6C328): Navy Blue, Dark Blue Green
  • On White (#FFFFFF): Navy Blue, Dark Blue Green
