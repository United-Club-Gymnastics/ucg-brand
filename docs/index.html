The links work for anyone signed in with a UCG Google account. “Make a copy” puts your own editable copy in your Drive; the templates themselves stay untouched.

How to use them

  1. Open the template you need and click Make a copy (or File → Make a copy). Save it in your team's folder.
  2. Replace every [bracketed] placeholder and delete the grey guidance lines.
  3. Use the built-in styles (Title, Heading 1, Heading 2, Normal text) from the style menu instead of formatting by hand, so headings stay on brand.
  4. Delete any section you don't need rather than leaving it empty.
