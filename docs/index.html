These are also in the Template gallery in Google Docs, Sheets, Slides and Forms, under United Club Gymnastics. “Use template” gives you your own copy (UCG Google accounts only); the templates themselves stay untouched.

How to use them

  1. In Google Docs, click Template gallery → United Club Gymnastics and pick the template (or click Use template above). Rename it and save it in your team's folder.
  2. Replace every [bracketed] placeholder and delete the guidance lines.
  3. Use the built-in styles (Title, Heading 1, Heading 2, Normal text) from the style menu instead of formatting by hand, so headings stay on brand.
  4. Delete any section you don't need rather than leaving it empty.

Google Forms

The form template

UCG Google Form Template

UCG header image, Saira Condensed headings and questions, Arial text and the Navy theme color. Collects respondents' emails.

Use template →Preview

These are also in the Template gallery in Google Docs, Sheets, Slides and Forms, under United Club Gymnastics. “Use template” gives you your own copy (UCG Google accounts only); the templates themselves stay untouched.

How to use it

  1. In Google Forms, click Template gallery → United Club Gymnastics → UCG Google Form Template (or Use template above).
  2. Rename the form and write a short description: what it's for, who should fill it in, and the deadline.
  3. Delete the sample question and add yours. Keep question titles short and put detail in the question description.
  4. Leave the theme as it is (header image, Saira Condensed, Arial, Navy). Swap the header image only for another UCG-branded one.
  5. The form collects respondents' emails. Turn that off in Settings if you don't need them, then Publish.
