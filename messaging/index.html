Tone of voice

Style: use clear, active language that speaks directly to members. Highlight belonging, participation, structure, and love of the sport. Don't overload people with acronyms, technical details, or formal language. Balance the credibility of a governing body with the warmth of a member-led community. Adjust depth for the audience (first-time adult gymnast, club leader, Nationals competitor, donor) while keeping the core message the same.

Welcoming

Warm, open, and encouraging. Speak to gymnasts like they already belong.

Credible

Clear, trusted, and organized. Approachable, but still a governing body.

Joyful

Energetic, positive, full of life, without becoming unserious.

Practical

Helpful, direct, easy to follow. People should know exactly what to do next.

About us (boilerplate)

Short

United Club Gymnastics (UCG) is a nonprofit, volunteer-run national organization for adult club gymnastics. Collegiate and community clubs compete in women's and men's artistic gymnastics and trampoline & tumbling, for the love of the sport.

Long

This is UCG. We create organized opportunities for gymnasts to keep participating, competing, and belonging beyond the traditional pathways of the sport. Through national events, member clubs, clear standards, and a supportive community, we help athletes stay connected to gymnastics in a way that fits their life and keeps their love of the sport alive.

Empathy statements

  • We understand how disorienting it can feel when the path forward in gymnastics suddenly disappears, even though the love for the sport is still there. You need a place where your stage of life, skill level, or competitive history does not determine whether you belong. We believe gymnastics should not have to end just because the traditional path does.
  • We understand how hard it can be to hold onto the joy of gymnastics when competition has often been tied to pressure, perfection, and constant advancement. You need an environment where effort, progress, and participation matter as much as the score. We believe competition should deepen your love for the sport, not take it away.

Example: member email

Hi Jordan,

Thanks for reaching out about getting involved with UCG. Whether you are coming from a college club, returning after time away, or looking for a new way to stay in the sport, there is a place for you here.

Our competitions bring together gymnasts of different ages, backgrounds, and experience levels. You can take your routines seriously, try something new, or simply enjoy being back on the floor. What matters most is that you still love the sport and want to be part of it.

Let us know what you are looking for, and we will help you find the right next step.

See you in the gym, The UCG Team

Brand story (long form)

There's a last time you walk up to the line. The last time you chalk your hands. The last time you grip the bar, step onto the beam, stand at the end of the runway.

Not because you stopped loving the sport, but because the path forward suddenly changed. The system moved on. The ladder ended. Life grew bigger. And the place where you once competed so naturally no longer had room for the person you were becoming.

For many gymnasts, the story seems to end there. But the pull of the sport doesn't disappear. Your body still knows how to move. Your mind still replays routines. You still feel that urge when you walk past a gym, hear the sound of the bar, see chalk in the air.

The love never left. The place just did.

A space where you don't have to prove you belong. Where the only requirement is the desire to step back onto the floor. Where routines can be serious, strong, or still in progress and every one of them is met with the same applause.

You run. You launch. You land. And before you even look up, you hear it. Not just cheers for the biggest skills, but for the effort and courage to keep coming back. Teammates, competitors, friends, all on the sidelines, all part of the same moment.

This is a sport built on individual performance. But here, it feels collective. This is where gymnastics becomes something you choose, not something that defines you. Where competition is fueled by love of the sport, not the fear of falling behind.

Because sometimes what you need most isn't another level to climb. It's an open floor. An open community. An open invitation to keep going.

UCG is that open place. It's a culture, a structure, and a home for those who still feel the pull of the sport and want to answer it, in their own way, on their own terms.

Here, the moment before the routine still matters. And there will always be a place to land.
