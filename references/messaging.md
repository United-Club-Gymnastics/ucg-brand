## Messaging themes

### For the Love of the Sport (core)
*Healthy competition fueled by joy, passion, and personal drive.*

UCG competition is serious, but not defined by pressure, fear, or status. Athletes train hard and compete because they want to, not because they have to. Applause is for courage, effort, and progress as much as for difficulty and execution. This creates a competitive culture that is intense, supportive, and sustainable. The love of the sport is the reason people show up, and the reason they stay.

Key phrases: Competing because you want to, not because you must · Passion over pressure · Effort deserves applause · Competition powered by joy · Love of the sport at every level · Serious about the sport, not the status · Train hard, love it harder · Courage over perfection · Progress worth cheering · Intense, supportive, and sustainable · Driven by passion, not pressure · Show up for the love, stay for the love · Where effort counts as much as execution · Competition that feeds the love · The reason people keep coming back

### A Place to Land
*A long-term competitive home beyond traditional gymnastics pathways.*

For many gymnasts, structured pathways eventually narrow. College eligibility ends. Elite tracks close. Life expands. UCG exists as the place where that trajectory does not have to stop. It is not a fallback. It is a landing: a stable, meaningful, and lasting place to continue growing in the sport. Athletes can push their skills, maintain their level, or explore something new, without the pressure of chasing the next rung. This is where gymnastics fits into a full life, not in place of one.

Key phrases: Where gymnasts land and stay · A home base for the next chapter of the sport · When the ladder ends, the floor is still here · A place built for the long run · Where your story in the sport continues · Somewhere to keep going · A stable place to grow · Not a fallback, a landing · Room for the long haul · Keep competing on your terms · The next chapter, not the end · A lasting place in the sport · Growth without chasing the next rung · Gymnastics that fits a full life · Still here when the path narrows

### Open Gym
*A welcoming competitive space where anyone can belong and participate.*

Open speaks to both access and attitude. UCG is open in format, open in structure, and open in spirit. Athletes of different ages, backgrounds, identities, and ability levels can compete side by side. Events are open. Pathways are open. Expectations are clear, but the door is not closed to anyone who feels the pull of the sport.

Key phrases: An open floor for whoever still feels the pull of the sport · Competition without closed doors · Open to different paths, shared love · An open invitation to keep going · Where the sport stays open to you · Open in format, structure, and spirit · All ages, all backgrounds, all levels · The door stays open · Come as you are, compete as you are · Belong from the first step · No gatekeeping, just gymnastics · Everyone on the same floor

### On the Same Team
*A culture of camaraderie across competitors, clubs, and communities.*

Gymnastics is an individual performance, but UCG feels collective. Athletes cheer for each other across clubs and divisions. Veterans encourage newcomers. Competitors celebrate competitors. The result is an environment where people feel seen, supported, and part of something larger than their score.

Key phrases: Competing together, even when routines are individual · A community built on shared experience · Rivals on paper, teammates in spirit · Everyone on the sidelines matters · A sport that feels like a team · Cheering across clubs and divisions · Individual routines, shared moment · Competitors who celebrate competitors · Veterans lifting up newcomers · Bigger than any one score · One floor, one community

### Good Spot
*Supportive structure that keeps competition safe, fair, and organized.*

UCG provides governance, standards, logistics, and leadership that make large-scale competition possible. Like a spotter in the gym, the organization is present, attentive, and ready to support, but never the center of attention, so athletes and clubs can focus on training, performing, and community.

Key phrases: Structure you can trust behind every meet · Support that makes the moment possible · Organized so athletes can focus on the floor · A steady hand behind the scenes · Like a spotter, always ready · Safe, fair, and organized · The framework behind the sport · Support without the spotlight · Behind the scenes so athletes shine

## Tone of voice

- **Welcoming** — warm, open, encouraging. Speak to gymnasts like they already belong.
- **Credible** — clear, trusted, organized. Approachable, but still a governing body.
- **Joyful** — energetic and positive, without becoming overly casual or unserious.
- **Practical** — helpful, direct, easy to follow. Reduce confusion; say exactly what to do next.

Style: use clear, active language that speaks directly to members. Highlight belonging, participation, structure, and love of the sport. Don't overload people with acronyms, technical details, or formal language. Balance the credibility of a governing body with the warmth of a member-led community. Adjust depth for the audience (first-time adult gymnast, club leader, Nationals competitor, donor) while keeping the core message the same.

## About us (boilerplate)

**Short** (written for this kit, not from the guide): United Club Gymnastics (UCG) is a nonprofit, volunteer-run national organization for adult club gymnastics. Collegiate and community clubs compete in women's and men's artistic gymnastics and trampoline & tumbling, for the love of the sport.

**Long:** This is UCG. We create organized opportunities for gymnasts to keep participating, competing, and belonging beyond the traditional pathways of the sport. Through national events, member clubs, clear standards, and a supportive community, we help athletes stay connected to gymnastics in a way that fits their life and keeps their love of the sport alive.

## Empathy statements

- We understand how disorienting it can feel when the path forward in gymnastics suddenly disappears, even though the love for the sport is still there. You need a place where your stage of life, skill level, or competitive history does not determine whether you belong. We believe gymnastics should not have to end just because the traditional path does.
- We understand how hard it can be to hold onto the joy of gymnastics when competition has often been tied to pressure, perfection, and constant advancement. You need an environment where effort, progress, and participation matter as much as the score. We believe competition should deepen your love for the sport, not take it away.

## Brand story (long form)

There's a last time you walk up to the line. The last time you chalk your hands. The last time you grip the bar, step onto the beam, stand at the end of the runway.

Not because you stopped loving the sport, but because the path forward suddenly changed. The system moved on. The ladder ended. Life grew bigger. And the place where you once competed so naturally no longer had room for the person you were becoming.

For many gymnasts, the story seems to end there. But the pull of the sport doesn't disappear. Your body still knows how to move. Your mind still replays routines. You still feel that urge when you walk past a gym, hear the sound of the bar, see chalk in the air.

The love never left. The place just did.

A space where you don't have to prove you belong. Where the only requirement is the desire to step back onto the floor. Where routines can be serious, strong, or still in progress and every one of them is met with the same applause.

You run. You launch. You land. And before you even look up, you hear it. Not just cheers for the biggest skills, but for the effort and courage to keep coming back. Teammates, competitors, friends, all on the sidelines, all part of the same moment.

This is a sport built on individual performance. But here, it feels collective. This is where gymnastics becomes something you choose, not something that defines you. Where competition is fueled by love of the sport, not the fear of falling behind.

Because sometimes what you need most isn't another level to climb. It's an open floor. An open community. An open invitation to keep going.

UCG is that open place. It's a culture, a structure, and a home for those who still feel the pull of the sport and want to answer it, in their own way, on their own terms.

Here, the moment before the routine still matters. And there will always be a place to land.
