## Post recipes (from the design guide)

- **Headline post:** solid color fill (Blue Green, Navy, Light Blue, Golden Yellow or Purple) with a huge Greed / Saira Condensed ALL CAPS headline in Navy (or White / Blue Green on Navy), small logo or mark in a corner, `@unitedclubgymnastics` handle small.
- **Photo post:** B&W photo, dynamic crop, White headline and graphics, optional Navy band or color overlay (B&W photo over a brand color, blend mode Screen) behind the text.
- **Event / discipline post:** event icons (MAG, WAG, T&T) large on a color block; "MAG · WAG · TNT" labels in condensed caps.
- **Carousel:** cover slide with the hook ("EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW FOR NATIONALS →"), one idea per slide (WHEN? WHERE? HOW TO SIGN UP?), arrow `→` bottom right, last slide with the link in bio / URL.
- **Grid:** alternate fills and photos across the 3-column grid so neighbors differ; keep type styles consistent.
- Rounded corners on photo frames and panels; a pill-shaped label for dates or categories.

## Expressive design (merch, campaigns, Nationals, big moments)

Expressive work can push color, scale and energy, but every piece keeps at least one **anchor**: the logo as drawn, the brand palette, or the brand type.

- **Green light:** merch and apparel, event graphics and signage for one event, social campaigns, Nationals and launch collateral.
- **Red light:** core logo lockups, official communications (policies, rules, registration info), permanent wayfinding, anything that must stay recognizable year to year.
- **Mixed pieces** (e.g. a Nationals hype post that also gives registration dates): the facts (dates, deadlines, links, prices) stay in core colors and plain, readable type; the expressive range can carry the background, pattern or decoration around them.
- You may recolor the mark within the palette, add a stroke, or use it big in the background. Never redraw or distort it.
- Expressive color range (for these pieces only): `#FFEF77` `#FF988A` `#C0E388` `#8BB7E3` `#336494` `#504A83`. Pair them with Navy or White and still meet AA contrast for any text.
- The **event pattern**: a repeating grid of "FOR THE LOVE OF THE SPORT" / "2026–2027 NATIONALS" lockups with the mark and "UCG", in Light Blue on Blue Green, for backgrounds and merch. Use the file as drawn: `assets/patterns/ucg-event-pattern-2026-27.svg` (or `.png`), taken from the design guide (p54).
- For anything big or public-facing (large print runs, apparel), have a designer review it.

## Captions

Lead with the moment or the useful fact, then the next step. 1–3 short paragraphs. End with a few hashtags: `#ucg` plus one or two specific ones (`#fortheloveofthesport`, `#adultgymnastics`, `#UCGNationals2027`). Examples in [`messaging.md`](messaging.md). Alt text for every image (Instagram → Advanced settings → Accessibility).

## Print

- Use CMYK / Pantone values from `brand.json` (Navy PMS 2377, Blue Green 551, Light Blue 551 at 50%, Purple 270, Red Orange 7416, Dark Blue Green 7477, Golden Yellow 123). Swatch file: `assets/palette/UCG_Colors.ase`.
- Vector logos (SVG, or the AI/EPS/PDF masters in the UCG toolkit) for anything printed large.
- File types: PDF for layouts, TIFF or high-res JPEG for photos, EPS/SVG for vector art.
- Small print (URL, phone, address) can be tiny Suisse / Arial, but keep it Navy on light or White on Navy.
