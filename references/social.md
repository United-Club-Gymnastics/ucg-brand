# Social graphics and print

Instagram (@unitedclubgymnastics), Facebook, flyers and posters. For merch, campaigns, event graphics and Nationals hype, also read [`expressive.md`](expressive.md).

| Format | Size |
|---|---|
| Instagram feed post | 1080 × 1350 (4:5) or 1080 × 1080 |
| Instagram / Facebook story, Reel cover | 1080 × 1920 (keep text clear of about 250px at the top, 380px at the bottom and 120px at the sides, where Instagram's buttons sit) |

## Post recipes (from the design guide)

- **Headline post:** solid color fill (Blue Green, Navy, Light Blue, Golden Yellow, Purple, Red Orange or Dark Blue Green) with a huge Greed / Saira Condensed ALL CAPS headline in Navy (or White / Light Blue / Golden Yellow on the dark fills), and the logo or mark. Small text follows the color pairs (on Blue Green it's Dark Blue Green).
- **Photo post:** B&W photo, dynamic crop, White headline and graphics, optional Navy band or color overlay (B&W photo over a brand color, blend mode Screen) behind the text.
- **Event / discipline post:** event icons (MAG, WAG, T&T) large on a color block; "MAG · WAG · TNT" labels in condensed caps.
- **Carousel:** cover slide with the hook ("EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW FOR NATIONALS →"), one idea per slide (WHEN? WHERE? HOW TO SIGN UP?), last slide with the link in bio / URL. An arrow `→` on each slide until the last tells people to swipe.
- **Grid:** mixing fills and photos across the 3-column grid keeps the feed lively; consistent type styles keep it recognizable.
- Rounded corners on photo frames and panels; a pill-shaped label for dates or categories.
- The `@unitedclubgymnastics` handle, set small, helps a graphic travel when it's shared.

## Captions

Lead with the moment or the useful fact, then the next step. Short paragraphs read best on a phone; the guide's examples run 3–4 short lines (MG p26). End with a few hashtags: `#ucg` plus one or two specific ones (`#fortheloveofthesport`, `#adultgymnastics`, `#UCGNationals2027`). Examples in [`messaging.md`](messaging.md). Alt text for every image (Instagram → Advanced settings → Accessibility).

## Print

- Use CMYK / Pantone values from `brand.json` (Navy PMS 2377, Blue Green 551, Light Blue 551 at 50%, Purple 270, Red Orange 7416, Dark Blue Green 7477, Golden Yellow 123). Swatch file: `assets/palette/UCG_Colors.ase`.
- Vector logos (SVG, or the AI/EPS/PDF masters in the UCG toolkit) for anything printed large.
- File types: PDF for layouts, TIFF or high-res JPEG for photos, EPS/SVG for vector art.
- Small print (URL, phone, address) can be tiny Suisse / Arial, as long as the pair works at small sizes (Navy on light, White or Light Blue on Navy).
