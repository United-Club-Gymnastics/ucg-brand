# Video, Reels and animation

## Mark animations (ready to use)

`assets/animation/` holds UCG's animated mark loops as MP4: the burst mark performing a skill (back flip, back handspring tuck, pommel horse, trampoline, synchro trampoline, uneven bars dismount, the three disciplines) plus an icon sequence. Each comes in:

- `_square` (1:1), `_vertical` (9:16, Reels/Stories), `_wide` (16:9), some with `_reel` cuts
- `_navy` (Light Blue mark on a Navy background) and `_light` (Navy mark on a Light Blue background)

Use them as intros, outros, transitions and loading moments. Transparent-background (alpha .mov) and GIF versions exist in the UCG brand toolkit for editors who need to composite.

## Reels and short videos

The fonts, palette, color pairs and captions are rules; the rest is what works well for UCG Reels.

- **Format:** 1080 × 1920 (30 fps is a good default). Keep text inside the center safe area (roughly 120px from the sides, 250px from the top, 380px from the bottom, where Instagram's UI sits).
- **Type:** Greed Condensed (licensed UCG volunteers; UCG's Greed license covers social video including Reels) or Saira Condensed Bold for titles, ALL CAPS; Suisse Intl or Arial for captions and small text. Burned-in captions on every video with speech.
- **Color:** palette backgrounds, with type that follows the color pairs on every frame (for example White, Light Blue or Golden Yellow on Navy; Navy on light colors). When a background changes color mid-transition behind an element of the same color, render the element twice (one per background) so there's never a same-color frame.
- **Footage:** real UCG events. B&W footage with White type (DG p17), or a color overlay (B&W footage over a brand color, blend mode Screen, DG p44), as long as the text on it meets the color pairs. Don't use footage showing old NAIGC branding.
- **Structure ideas:** a hook in the first second (a big headline or a striking skill), one idea per screen, and an end card with the mark animation, the next step and `@unitedclubgymnastics`.
- **Motion ideas:** confident and athletic. Section titles can slam in with a single overshoot; reading holds stay still; the next beat starts as soon as the last one settles. The mark and icons can tumble and flip, as the mark animations do.
- **Music:** licensed or platform library tracks only.

## Livestream and broadcast graphics

Lower thirds, scoreboards and overlays for Nationals or meet streams. **Use Saira Condensed + Arial here**, since UCG's Greed license doesn't include the broadcast "Video" tier. A look that works: Navy bars with White / Light Blue text and a Blue Green accent rule, and the mark at small size.
